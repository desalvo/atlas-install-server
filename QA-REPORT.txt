Self-update behavior
--------------------
- disabled by default;
- persisted when enabled;
- can be controlled with --self-update / --no-self-update;
- ATLAS_WIZARD_AUTO_UPDATE=1 enables it for unattended execution;
- downloaded wizard is installed only if scripts/atlas-install-k8s-wizard.sh.sha256 matches;
- replacement is atomic and becomes active on the following execution.
